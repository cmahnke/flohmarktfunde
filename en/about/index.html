About this blog

This blog shows interesting artifacts that I have acquired on my visits to flea markets, in antique shops and online in and around Göttingen. The objects shown are only a selection; media such as books, magazines, postcards, posters, records, tapes, video games and films as well as everyday objects are usually not filed.

For each object some additional information is offered, among other things the price at which it was acquired — this does not mean that the object in question is for sale — as well as the location. Where no information is stored, there is currently still placeholder text (Lorem Ipsum ).

My collecting areas

  • Old media

    • Playback devices
